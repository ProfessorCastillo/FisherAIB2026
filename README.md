# Fisher AI in Business Conference 2026

Web app for the 2026 AI in Business Conference at the Max M. Fisher College of Business,
The Ohio State University — **October 14–16, 2026**.

**Live site:** https://professorcastillo.github.io/FisherAIB2026/conference-main.html

## What's here

- **Schedule** — the full three-day program: the industry track (Oct 14) and the academic
  track (Oct 15–16), with keynotes, breakout sessions, rooms, and times.
- **Presentation search** — find talks by presenter, title, discipline, track, day, or Best
  Paper award category, with a deep link into each talk's place on the schedule.
- **Knowledge Constellation** — an interactive map of accepted presentations clustered by
  topic, with readable "who to meet and why" recommendations. Only presentations whose authors
  opted in appear here.

## About

A static site (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) served from GitHub Pages — no server, database, or
runtime AI. The program and constellation data are generated offline and published as JSON.
