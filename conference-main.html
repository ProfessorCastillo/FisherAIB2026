iOS Installation (Safari/Chrome):

  1. Open this website in Safari or Chrome
  2. Tap the Share button (☐↗) at the bottom (Safari) or address bar (Chrome).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."
  4. Tap "Add" to confirm

The app will appear on your home screen with the conference logo!

Android Installation (Chrome):

Follow these steps to install the app on your mobile home screen for easy access.

  1. Open this website in your Chrome browser.
  2. Tap the browser menu (⋮) in the top right.
  3. Select "Add to home screen."
